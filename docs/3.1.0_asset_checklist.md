## 8. Rules for everything you add

- **Licence:** only use media you own or that allows commercial use. Keep a note of each source.
- **People:** do not use identifiable photos of real people without written permission. This matters most for Vineward screenshots (ministry names and spiritual-progress data).
- **Size budget:** hero image under 300 KB, hero video under 6 MB, orbit video under 8 MB.
- **Hero legibility:** dark, low-contrast footage only, since white headline text sits on top.
- **Accessibility:** every video should be understandable without sound. Add captions to anything with speech.
